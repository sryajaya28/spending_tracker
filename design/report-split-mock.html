What is real, what is new

  • Real: hero, cards, rows, chips, buttons, tabs, budget bars, donut, trend, Money Flow and the bottom bar are the app's own markup and styles.
  • New: the Transactions | Analysis tabs (the Portfolio tab component), the Today card header, the missing-days card, and the bottom tab renamed Report.
  • Removed from Overview: the budget line and bar, Money Flow, Spending, Category trend.
  • Rebuild after the app changes: node design/build_report_mock.js.

Rules behind frame 1

  • A missing day is one of the last 7 days (today excluded) with no transaction of any kind. Recurring entries count.
  • Days before the first transaction are never flagged.
  • Nothing to add is remembered on this device only (no database change).
  • Add, or a dashed day in the strip, opens Add transaction dated to that day.
  • No missing days: the card is not shown at all (frame 4).
  • Nothing recorded today: the whole Today section becomes one prompt with a single Add transaction button (frame 4).

Please look at

  • Bottom bar: Overview, Report, Add, Budgets, Portfolio. Budgets stays its own tab.
  • Analysis shows budget progress only (no add or edit), then Spending, Category trend and Money Flow. Is a read-only budget summary what you want, or none?
  • Report has no subtitle line and no summary line. The month stays in the month picker.
