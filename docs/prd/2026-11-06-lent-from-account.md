# PRD: Money lent comes out of an account (owner brief 2026-11-06)

Status line: PRD v1 | owner approved: pending | Figma: pending (Designer) | Branch: prd/lent-from-account | QA report: pending | Lane: Tier 3 (net worth, balances, money records; may need a migration)

1. **Problem and goal.** Owner, 2026-11-06: "When i want to add new Lent, it should specify which account used to Lent" and "Record the transaction on Lent in Transaction page also as Transfers with category Lent". Today "Add money lent" asks for who, amount, currency, due date and note, but not where the money came from, so the account balance does not drop and Transactions shows nothing, while a repayment already arrives as a transfer-in with category Lent Repayment. Lending should be the mirror: money leaves an account and appears in Transactions.
2. **Scope.** In: the Add money lent form (account field), a linked transfer-out transaction with category **Lent**, editing and deleting a lent record keeps that transaction in step, a new default category "Lent" (English and Indonesian), Transactions list/detail wording, sample data. Out: repayment flow (unchanged), loans you owe, budgets, changing old records, bank sync.
3. **User stories.** As a user lending money I pick the account it came from, so that my balances stay true. As a user reviewing Transactions I see the loan as a transfer out with category Lent, so that my history shows where the money went.
4. **Acceptance checks.**
   1. Given Add money lent, when I open it, then there is a required "From account" field listing my savings accounts (not cards or investments), with the last-used account preselected.
   2. Given I save a loan of Rp 500.000 from BCA, then BCA's balance drops by Rp 500.000, the Lent tab shows Rp 500.000 owed to me, and net worth is unchanged.
   3. Given I save it, then Transactions shows one transfer out on BCA, note "<name>", category **Lent**, dated the lent date, and it is not counted as spending in budgets, Money Flow or category charts.
   4. Given the account holds less than the amount, when I save, then the same "doesn't have enough balance" message as loan payments blocks it (a credit-card-type account is not offered).
   5. Given the account currency differs from the currency chosen, then the currency is locked to the account's currency (the Currency field follows the account).
   6. Given I edit a lent record's amount, account, date or name, then the linked transaction changes with it; given I delete the lent record, then its linked transaction is deleted and the balance is restored.
   7. Given a lent record created before this change (no account), then it still shows as before, with no transaction, and can be edited without being forced to pick an account.
   8. Given a repayment is recorded, then it works as today (transfer in, category Lent Repayment, linked to the lent record).
   9. Given the Viewer role, then no add or edit controls appear; given an Editor edits a lent record created by someone else, then the linked transaction follows the lent record's rules (see rule 2 below).
   10. Given Bahasa Indonesia, dark mode and phone width, then the form, the category and the chip read correctly.
5. **Edge cases and rules.** (1) Lent date defaults to today; a past date is allowed, a future date is not (like payments). (2) Household: an Editor may edit any lent record (as today); the linked transaction is changed only through the lent screen, so the rule "Editors change only their own transactions" is not hit; the transaction opened directly from Transactions shows "managed from Money lent" and is read-only there. (3) Archived or deleted account: a loan whose account is archived keeps its transaction; an account that holds a lent transaction follows the existing delete guard. (4) Offline and sync: same as other records; one save writes both rows.
6. **Data and privacy.** Likely one migration: a link from the lent record to its transaction (as repayments already have `txn_id`) plus the new default category; backwards compatible, old rows untouched; Tier 3 review applies. Nothing private goes to analytics (no names, amounts).
7. **Analytics.** Extend `add_lent` with `has_account: true` (no new event); `update_lent` and delete unchanged. Success: share of new lent records that have an account, target above 90% in the first month (the rest are unrecorded cash).
8. **Design notes.** Add money lent form: new "From account" field above the amount (reuse the account picker of the loan payment form), currency follows the account; list row in Transactions as transfer out with the Lent category icon; Figma by the Designer in light, dark, phone; Indonesian strings.
9. **Open questions (recommended default in bold).** (a) Make the account **required** for new lent records (alternative: optional, as it is for repayments today)? (b) Show a **short note** in the form when the account is the only thing saved ("This also adds a transfer out")? default no. (c) Old lent records: **leave as they are** (alternative: offer a one-time "link an account" action). (d) Fast lane for the design (**yes, small change**; the data side still goes through Tier 3 review).
10. **Release plan.** Tier 3, ships alone; migration to staging first, then production through the deploy workflow with the owner's approval; QA real-account check of the balance and net worth numbers; PostHog `add_lent` with `has_account`.
