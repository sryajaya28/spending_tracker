Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Stiver is a personal finance app for tracking spending, budgets, savings, investments and loans. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not get access to your Google Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else.
  • Password: if you sign up with a password, it is stored only as a secure hash. We cannot read it.
  • The data you enter: wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, investments, loans and similar records, plus your settings (currency, language, theme).
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address and browser type, which our hosting and sign-in providers process to keep the service secure and working.
  • Usage statistics: which screens are opened and which actions are used (for example "a transaction was saved"), linked to a random account id. We never send amounts, names, notes, your email or the content of your records. We do not record your screen, and Do Not Track is respected.
  • On your device: we store a sign-in session and a few preferences in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the app and show you your own data on your devices.
  • To sign you in, send confirmation and password-reset emails, and keep your account secure.
  • To fix problems and prevent abuse.
  • To understand which parts of the app are used, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your financial records to profile you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each record is tied to your account, and the database only returns your own records to you. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and turn on two-step verification on your email and Google accounts.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all its data at any time using the contact details below, and we will do so.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Stiver is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: please use the support email shown on the sign-in screen.
